• When to CLASSIFY:
    • Ask yourself "Is this a single magic card that can be identified"
    • Crops that are very zoomed in but you can identify the card is OKAY
    • Cards in other languages are OKAY to classify
    • Cards with alternate arts are OKAY. Just use whatever art comes up in the search
  • When to DELETE:
    • Delete all tokens
    • Anything that is not a face up magic card
    • Bad crops, crops that are too zoomed out and not focused on a card
  • When to say "Im not sure":
    • The card is valid and should NOT be deleted
    • You can't figure out what the card is
