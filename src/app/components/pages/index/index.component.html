Welcome to Table Stream! A platform that lets you connect, share your webcam, and enjoy games like Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, and Yu-Gi-Oh! with friends online.

How It Works

Table Stream allows you to join or create games for different formats like Commander, Standard, Modern, and more. Stream your table using your webcam and microphone to play with your own card collection.

Our platform will soon support other popular card games like Lorcana. Expand your gaming experience and connect with players worldwide!

Game Features

  • 2-6 player games
  • Search and share cards in-game
  • Real-time audio and video streaming
  • Fun reactions and soundboards
  • Spectator support
  • Password protected games
  • Game state tracking such as:
    • Life totals
    • Prize Cards
    • Commander Damages
    • Energy
    • Poison
    • Monarch
    • Turn Timers
    • Cities Blessing

Planned Features

  • Public server list
  • Much more!

No Account Needed

Table Stream is completely free to use. No account or email registration is required. Just jump in and start playing!
