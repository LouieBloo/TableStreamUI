Important Notice

Your IP Address May Be Exposed

Please be aware that when using this application for peer-to-peer (P2P) video streaming, your IP address may be visible to other users. This is a common aspect of P2P technology, as it allows direct connections between users for streaming.

To protect your privacy:

  • Consider using a Virtual Private Network (VPN) to help mask your IP address.
  • Be cautious about sharing personal information while using this application.

By continuing to use this app, you acknowledge that you understand the potential privacy implications.
