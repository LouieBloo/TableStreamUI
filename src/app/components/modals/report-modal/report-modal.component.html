Known Bugs and Issues

  • If you are a spectator, do not have a camera, or have denied the camera permission, you may not see other players cameras on refresh. To fix, go back to the login page and rejoin.
  • Clicking to search for a card on a stream is in alpha and has a low succeess rate. Try bringing the card closer to the camera for better results.
